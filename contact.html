About StudySpot Haarlem

A simple student project to help you find a suitable place to study in Haarlem.

Why was it created?

StudySpot Haarlem was created to make it easier for students to find and compare suitable study places in Haarlem.

Who is it for?

The website is designed for students who need a place outside their home to study, read, work on assignments or prepare for exams.

What information does it provide?

The website focuses on information such as Wi-Fi, quietness, power outlets, seating, cost and opening hours.
